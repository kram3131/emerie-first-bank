# Emerie First Bank - Consumer Lending

## Overview

Emerie First Bank offers a range of personal lending products to help customers finance major purchases, consolidate debt, and access the equity in their homes. All consumer loans feature local underwriting and decision-making, with most applications receiving a decision within one to two business days.

## Personal Loans

### Unsecured Personal Loan

This is a fixed-rate, fixed-term loan that does not require collateral. It can be used for debt consolidation, home improvements, medical expenses, vacations, or other personal needs.

Loan amounts range from $2,500 to $50,000. Terms are available from 12 to 60 months. Current rates start at 8.49% APR for well-qualified borrowers with excellent credit. The maximum rate is 15.99% APR. Rates are determined by creditworthiness, loan amount, and term length.

There is no origination fee. There is no prepayment penalty, so borrowers can pay off the loan early at any time without additional cost.

Monthly payments are fixed for the life of the loan. An application fee of $0 applies. The bank does charge a $25.00 late payment fee if a payment is more than 10 days past due.

### Secured Personal Loan

This loan is secured by a deposit account at Emerie First Bank (savings account or CD). Because the loan is backed by collateral, rates are lower than an unsecured personal loan.

Loan amounts range from $1,000 to the value of the pledged deposit minus 10%. Terms are available from 12 to 60 months. Current rates start at 4.99% APR. The rate is typically 2.00% to 3.00% above the rate being earned on the pledged deposit.

The pledged deposit account remains in the customer's name and continues to earn interest, but withdrawals are restricted until the loan is repaid.

## Auto Loans

### New Auto Loan

Emerie First Bank finances new vehicles (current model year and one year prior) purchased from licensed dealerships.

Loan amounts range from $10,000 to $100,000. Terms are available from 24 to 72 months. Current rates start at 5.49% APR for 24 to 48 month terms and 5.99% APR for 60 to 72 month terms. Maximum financing is up to 100% of the purchase price plus tax, title, and license fees.

Rate is based on credit score, loan term, and loan-to-value ratio. A $150 documentation fee applies at closing. There is no prepayment penalty.

### Used Auto Loan

Financing is available for used vehicles up to 7 model years old with fewer than 100,000 miles, purchased from a licensed dealership or through a private party sale.

Loan amounts range from $5,000 to $75,000. Terms are available from 24 to 60 months. Current rates start at 6.29% APR for 24 to 48 month terms and 6.79% APR for 60 month terms. Maximum financing is up to 90% of NADA retail value for dealership purchases and 80% of NADA retail value for private party purchases.

A $150 documentation fee applies at closing. There is no prepayment penalty.

### Auto Loan Refinance

Customers who have an existing auto loan with another lender can refinance with Emerie First Bank. The vehicle must be no more than 7 model years old with fewer than 100,000 miles. Refinance rates start at 5.99% APR and are based on the same factors as a standard auto loan. There is no application fee for refinancing.

## RV and Recreational Vehicle Loans

Emerie First Bank finances new and used recreational vehicles including motorhomes, travel trailers, fifth wheels, and camper vans.

### New RV Loan

Loan amounts range from $10,000 to $250,000. Terms are available from 24 to 180 months depending on the loan amount. Current rates start at 6.49% APR for terms up to 60 months and 6.99% APR for terms of 61 to 180 months. Maximum financing is up to 90% of the purchase price for new RVs purchased from a licensed dealer.

### Used RV Loan

Financing is available for used RVs up to 10 model years old purchased from a licensed dealer or through a private party sale. Loan amounts range from $10,000 to $150,000. Terms are available from 24 to 144 months depending on the loan amount. Current rates start at 7.29% APR. Maximum financing is up to 80% of NADA retail value.

### RV Loan General Information

A $150 documentation fee applies at closing for all RV loans. There is no prepayment penalty. For loan amounts above $50,000, the bank may require a marine and RV survey or inspection. Proof of insurance with comprehensive and collision coverage is required before closing. The RV does not need to be the borrower's primary residence.

## Home Equity Products

### Home Equity Loan (Fixed Rate)

A home equity loan provides a lump sum of funds secured by the borrower's primary residence. This product is commonly used for home improvements, debt consolidation, or major expenses.

Loan amounts range from $10,000 to $250,000. The maximum combined loan-to-value ratio (including the first mortgage) is 80%. Terms are available from 60 to 180 months. Current rates start at 6.99% APR for well-qualified borrowers.

Closing costs typically range from $500 to $2,500 depending on the loan amount and property location. An appraisal is required and is included in closing costs. There is no prepayment penalty. Interest may be tax-deductible if the funds are used for home improvements. Borrowers should consult a tax advisor.

### Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC)

A HELOC provides a revolving line of credit secured by the borrower's primary residence. Borrowers can draw funds as needed during the draw period and only pay interest on the amount borrowed.

Credit lines range from $10,000 to $250,000. The maximum combined loan-to-value ratio is 80%. The draw period is 10 years, followed by a 20-year repayment period. During the draw period, minimum payments are interest only. During the repayment period, payments include principal and interest.

The current variable rate is Prime Rate plus 0.50%, with a floor of 5.50% APR and a ceiling of 18.00% APR. As of March 2026, the Prime Rate is 7.50%, making the current HELOC rate 8.00% APR. The rate adjusts monthly based on changes to the Prime Rate as published in the Wall Street Journal.

There is a $75 annual fee, waived in the first year. Closing costs are similar to the home equity loan. Borrowers can convert a portion of the outstanding balance to a fixed rate at any time through the rate lock option, with a $100 rate lock fee.

## General Lending Information

All consumer loan applications require a completed application, proof of income (two most recent pay stubs or tax returns for self-employed borrowers), and a valid government-issued photo ID. Auto loans also require the vehicle purchase agreement or title information. Home equity products require property information and a current mortgage statement.

Credit decisions are based on credit history, income, debt-to-income ratio, and collateral value where applicable. Emerie First Bank generally looks for a debt-to-income ratio of 43% or less for consumer loans and home equity products.

Customers can apply for any consumer loan online at www.emeriefirstbank.com, by phone at (512) 930-4500, or in person at any branch location.
