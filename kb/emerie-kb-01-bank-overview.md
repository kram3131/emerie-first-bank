# Emerie First Bank - Bank Overview & General Info

## About Emerie First Bank

Emerie First Bank is a community-focused regional bank headquartered in Georgetown, Texas. Founded in 1987, the bank has served Central Texas families and businesses for nearly four decades. Emerie First Bank is a member FDIC institution, and all deposits are insured up to the standard $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.

The bank's routing number is 111400527.

Emerie First Bank operates under a Texas state banking charter and is regulated by the Texas Department of Banking and the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

## Mission

Emerie First Bank exists to strengthen the communities we serve by providing reliable, personal banking backed by local decision-making. We believe that when your banker knows your name and understands your goals, better outcomes follow.

## Leadership

Sarah Langford serves as President and CEO. The bank employs approximately 185 team members across its five locations. Emerie First Bank is locally owned and governed by a board of directors made up of Central Texas business and civic leaders.

## Community Involvement

Emerie First Bank is an active supporter of local organizations including the Georgetown Chamber of Commerce, Round Rock ISD Education Foundation, Habitat for Humanity of Williamson County, the Williamson County Children's Advocacy Center, and numerous youth sports programs across the region. The bank contributes more than $300,000 annually to community organizations and offers employees 16 hours of paid volunteer time each year.
