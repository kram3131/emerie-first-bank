# Emerie First Bank - Personal Savings, Money Market and CDs

## Overview

Emerie First Bank offers several options for customers looking to save and grow their money. These include traditional savings accounts, a high-yield savings option, money market accounts, and certificates of deposit. All deposit accounts are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.

## Savings Accounts

### EFB Standard Savings

This is a straightforward savings account with a low opening deposit and no monthly fee when basic requirements are met. The monthly maintenance fee is $5.00, waived when the account maintains a $300 minimum daily balance or the account holder is under 18.

The current annual percentage yield is 0.25% APY on all balances. Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly.

The minimum opening deposit is $25. There is no limit on the number of deposits. Federal Regulation D withdrawal limits were lifted in 2020, but Emerie First Bank allows up to six free withdrawals or transfers per monthly statement cycle. Each withdrawal beyond six is subject to a $2.00 excess transaction fee.

### EFB High-Yield Savings

This account offers a significantly higher rate for customers who maintain larger balances. There is no monthly maintenance fee as long as the account holds a $5,000 minimum daily balance. If the balance falls below $5,000, a $12.00 monthly fee applies.

The current rate structure is tiered:
- Balances from $0 to $4,999: 0.25% APY
- Balances from $5,000 to $24,999: 3.90% APY
- Balances from $25,000 to $99,999: 4.10% APY
- Balances of $100,000 and above: 4.25% APY

Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly. The minimum opening deposit is $1,000. The same six-free-withdrawal policy applies, with a $2.00 fee per excess transaction.

## Money Market Accounts

### EFB Money Market

The Money Market account combines higher earning potential with limited check-writing ability. It comes with a checkbook (no debit card) and allows up to six checks or transfers per statement cycle.

The monthly maintenance fee is $10.00, waived with a $10,000 minimum daily balance.

The current rate structure is tiered:
- Balances from $0 to $9,999: 0.30% APY
- Balances from $10,000 to $49,999: 3.75% APY
- Balances from $50,000 to $99,999: 4.00% APY
- Balances of $100,000 and above: 4.15% APY

Interest is compounded daily and credited monthly. The minimum opening deposit is $2,500. Withdrawals beyond six per statement cycle are subject to a $10.00 excess transaction fee.

## Certificates of Deposit (CDs)

### Standard CDs

Emerie First Bank offers fixed-rate CDs with terms ranging from 3 months to 60 months. The minimum deposit for all CDs is $1,000. Interest can be paid monthly to a linked account or compounded and added to the CD balance at maturity.

Current CD rates (as of August 2026):
- 3-month CD: 4.50% APY
- 6-month CD: 4.60% APY
- 12-month CD: 4.40% APY
- 18-month CD: 4.25% APY
- 24-month CD: 4.10% APY
- 36-month CD: 3.95% APY
- 48-month CD: 3.80% APY
- 60-month CD: 3.70% APY

Rates are fixed at the time of opening and do not change during the term.

### Jumbo CDs

Jumbo CDs require a minimum deposit of $100,000 and offer a rate premium of 0.10% APY above the corresponding standard CD rate for each term.

### CD Renewal and Early Withdrawal

CDs automatically renew at maturity for the same term length at the current posted rate. Customers have a 10-day grace period after maturity to withdraw funds, change the term, or add to the balance without penalty.

Early withdrawal penalties apply if funds are removed before maturity:
- Terms of 12 months or less: penalty equal to 90 days of interest
- Terms of 13 to 36 months: penalty equal to 180 days of interest
- Terms of 37 to 60 months: penalty equal to 365 days of interest

Early withdrawal penalties may reduce the principal balance if earned interest is insufficient to cover the penalty.

### IRA CDs

Emerie First Bank offers Traditional IRA and Roth IRA CDs with the same terms and rates as standard CDs. The minimum deposit for IRA CDs is $1,000. Contributions and distributions are subject to IRS rules and limits. Customers should consult a tax advisor for guidance on IRA contributions, withdrawals, and required minimum distributions. The bank does not provide tax advice.

## Savings Tools

All savings and money market account holders have access to automatic transfer tools through online banking. Customers can schedule recurring transfers from checking to savings on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly basis. The Emerie First Bank mobile app also includes a round-up savings feature that rounds debit card purchases to the nearest dollar and transfers the difference to a linked savings account.

## How to Open a Savings Account or CD

Customers can open any savings account, money market account, or CD online at www.emeriefirstbank.com, through the mobile app, or at any branch. The same identification requirements apply as with checking accounts: a valid government-issued photo ID and Social Security number or ITIN. Funds can be deposited via transfer from an existing account, check, or cash at a branch.
