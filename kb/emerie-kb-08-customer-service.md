# Emerie First Bank - Customer Service and Policies

## Overview

Emerie First Bank is committed to providing responsive, personal customer service across all channels. This section covers common service requests, bank policies, and how to get help.

## Contacting Customer Service

General Customer Service: (512) 930-4500
Toll-Free: 1-888-364-7428
Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM CT. Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM CT. Closed Sunday.

Secure Messaging: Available through the mobile app and online banking during all hours. Responses are typically provided within one business day.

In-Person: Visit any branch during lobby hours for assistance.

## Wire Transfers

Emerie First Bank can send and receive both domestic and international wire transfers.

Domestic outgoing wire transfers can be initiated in person at any branch or by calling customer service. Online wire requests are available for Business Plus and Business Premium Checking account holders. The cutoff time for same-day processing is 3:00 PM CT. Wires requested after the cutoff are processed the next business day.

Domestic incoming wires are typically posted to the account within the same business day if received before 4:00 PM CT.

International outgoing wire transfers must be initiated in person at a branch. Processing time is typically 1 to 3 business days depending on the destination country and intermediary banks involved. International wires are sent in U.S. dollars unless the customer requests a specific foreign currency.

International incoming wires are posted to the account once received and converted to U.S. dollars at the prevailing exchange rate at the time of posting.

Wire transfer fees are listed in the Fee Schedule (Module 9).

## Cashiers Checks and Official Bank Checks

Cashiers checks are available at any branch for a fee of $8.00 each. Premium Checking account holders receive unlimited free cashiers checks. If a cashiers check is lost or stolen, the customer must complete an indemnity agreement and wait 90 days before a replacement can be issued.

## Notary Services

Free notary services are available at all branch locations during lobby hours for Emerie First Bank customers. Notary services are provided on a walk-in basis, but customers are encouraged to call ahead to confirm a notary is available at their preferred branch.

## Safe Deposit Boxes

Safe deposit boxes are available at the Georgetown Main, Round Rock, and Temple branch locations. Box sizes and annual rental fees are as follows:

Small (3 x 5 inches): $50 per year.
Medium (5 x 10 inches): $100 per year.
Large (10 x 10 inches): $175 per year.

Premium Checking account holders receive one free small safe deposit box (subject to availability). Rental fees are billed annually to the customers checking account. Access to safe deposit boxes is available during lobby hours. Two keys are provided at the time of rental. Lost key replacement costs $25 per key. If both keys are lost, the box must be drilled at the customers expense, which typically costs $150 to $250.

## Funds Availability Policy

Emerie First Bank funds availability policy complies with Federal Reserve Regulation CC.

Direct deposits, wire transfers, and electronic payments are available on the day of deposit. Cash deposits made in person are available on the day of deposit. Cash deposits made at an ATM are available the next business day.

For check deposits, the first $225 is available on the first business day after the day of deposit. The remaining funds from local checks are generally available on the second business day. Checks drawn on out-of-area banks may take up to five business days.

The bank may place an extended hold on deposits in certain circumstances, including new accounts (open less than 30 days), deposits exceeding $5,525, redeposited checks that were previously returned, and accounts with repeated overdrafts. When an extended hold is placed, the customer will be notified at the time of deposit with the date the funds will become available.

## Dispute Resolution and Error Claims

If a customer identifies an unauthorized transaction or error on their account, they should notify the bank as soon as possible. For debit card disputes, the customer must contact the bank within 60 days of the statement date on which the error appeared.

The bank will investigate the claim and provide provisional credit within 10 business days of receiving the dispute if the investigation has not been completed. The investigation may take up to 45 calendar days (90 calendar days for point-of-sale transactions, foreign transactions, and new accounts).

The customer will be notified in writing of the outcome. If the investigation determines no error occurred, the provisional credit will be reversed, and the customer will be given an explanation and the right to request documentation.

To file a dispute, customers can call (512) 930-4500, visit any branch, or submit a dispute through secure messaging in the mobile app.

## Fraud Prevention

Emerie First Bank monitors accounts for suspicious activity using automated fraud detection systems. If suspicious activity is detected on a debit card, the bank may temporarily block the card and attempt to contact the customer by phone or text to verify the transaction.

Customers should never share their online banking credentials, debit card PIN, or one-time verification codes with anyone. The bank will never call, email, or text customers and ask for their full account number, password, or PIN.

If a customer believes they have been a victim of fraud, they should call the 24/7 fraud line at 1-888-364-7430 immediately. The bank will freeze the affected accounts, issue new cards, and begin an investigation.

## Account Closures

Customers can close an account by visiting any branch in person or by calling customer service. Written requests to close an account can be mailed to the Georgetown Main branch. The bank cannot close accounts through secure messaging or email for security reasons.

Any remaining balance will be disbursed by check mailed to the address on file or transferred to another Emerie First Bank account. All outstanding checks and automatic payments should be resolved before closing an account. If the account has a negative balance at the time of closure, the customer is responsible for paying the amount owed.

There is no fee to close a checking or savings account that has been open for more than 90 days. Accounts closed within the first 90 days are subject to a $25.00 early closure fee.

## Stop Payments

Customers can place a stop payment on a check by calling customer service or through online banking. The stop payment fee is $30.00 per item. A stop payment order is effective for six months and can be renewed. The customer must provide the check number, amount, date, and payee name. Stop payments cannot be placed on cashiers checks.

## Deceased Account Holder

When an account holder passes away, a family member or legal representative should notify the bank as soon as possible. The bank will require a certified copy of the death certificate. Joint accounts with right of survivorship will transfer to the surviving account holder. Individual accounts and other joint account types will be handled in accordance with the account agreement and applicable Texas and federal law. The banks customer service team will guide the family through the required steps.

## Compliance and Regulatory Information

Emerie First Bank complies with all applicable federal and state banking regulations including the Bank Secrecy Act, USA PATRIOT Act, Community Reinvestment Act, Truth in Lending Act, Equal Credit Opportunity Act, Fair Housing Act, and the Electronic Fund Transfer Act.

The bank may be required to file Currency Transaction Reports for cash transactions exceeding $10,000 and Suspicious Activity Reports when warranted. Customers are prohibited from structuring transactions to avoid reporting requirements.

Emerie First Bank most recent Community Reinvestment Act performance evaluation is available for review at the Georgetown Main branch.
