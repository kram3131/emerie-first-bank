# Emerie First Bank - Business Banking

## Overview

Emerie First Bank provides a full range of banking services for small and mid-sized businesses across Central Texas. Our business banking team works with sole proprietors, partnerships, LLCs, corporations, and nonprofits. All business deposit accounts are FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per ownership category.

The Business Banking Department can be reached at (512) 930-4560.

## Business Checking Accounts

### EFB Business Basic Checking

This is a no-fee business checking account designed for small businesses and sole proprietors with modest transaction volumes.

There is no monthly maintenance fee. The account includes 200 free transactions per month (checks, deposits, and electronic transactions combined). Each transaction beyond 200 is $0.25. Cash deposits up to $5,000 per month are processed at no charge. Cash deposits beyond $5,000 are subject to a $1.50 per $1,000 cash handling fee.

The account includes a Visa business debit card, online and mobile banking, mobile deposit, and free Bill Pay. The minimum opening deposit is $100.

### EFB Business Plus Checking

This account is built for growing businesses with higher transaction volumes and a need for more features. The monthly maintenance fee is $20.00, waived with a $10,000 minimum daily balance.

The account includes 500 free transactions per month. Each transaction beyond 500 is $0.20. Cash deposits up to $10,000 per month are processed at no charge. Cash deposits beyond $10,000 are subject to a $1.50 per $1,000 cash handling fee.

In addition to everything in Business Basic Checking, this account adds free incoming domestic wire transfers, two free outgoing domestic wire transfers per month, and earnings credit that can offset monthly service charges based on the average collected balance.

The minimum opening deposit is $500. Interest is earned at 0.10% APY on balances over $10,000.

### EFB Business Premium Checking

This is our top-tier business checking account for established businesses with significant deposit balances and complex banking needs. The monthly maintenance fee is $35.00, waived with a $50,000 minimum daily balance or $100,000 in combined business deposit and loan balances.

The account includes unlimited transactions with no per-item fees. Cash deposits up to $25,000 per month are free. Cash deposits beyond $25,000 are subject to a $1.00 per $1,000 cash handling fee.

Additional features include unlimited free domestic wire transfers (incoming and outgoing), a dedicated business relationship manager, free positive pay fraud protection, and priority processing on loan applications.

The minimum opening deposit is $1,000. Interest is earned at 0.20% APY on balances over $25,000.

### EFB Nonprofit Checking

This account is available to registered 501(c)(3) organizations. There is no monthly maintenance fee and no minimum balance requirement.

The account includes 300 free transactions per month and $10,000 in free cash deposits per month. Per-item and cash handling fees beyond those thresholds match the Business Plus Checking schedule.

The minimum opening deposit is $100.

## Business Savings and Money Market

### Business Savings

A straightforward savings account for setting aside business reserves. The monthly maintenance fee is $5.00, waived with a $1,000 minimum daily balance. Current rate is 0.30% APY on all balances. The minimum opening deposit is $100.

### Business Money Market

Higher earning potential for businesses with larger reserve balances. The monthly maintenance fee is $15.00, waived with a $25,000 minimum daily balance.

Current tiered rates:
- Balances from $0 to $24,999: 0.30% APY
- Balances from $25,000 to $99,999: 3.50% APY
- Balances from $100,000 to $249,999: 3.75% APY
- Balances of $250,000 and above: 3.90% APY

The account includes check-writing ability (up to six checks or transfers per statement cycle). The minimum opening deposit is $5,000.

## Business Lending

### Business Line of Credit

A revolving credit line for managing cash flow, covering seasonal expenses, or handling unexpected costs. Credit lines range from $10,000 to $250,000. The rate is variable, currently Prime Rate plus 1.00% to Prime Rate plus 3.00% depending on creditworthiness and collateral. As of March 2026, that puts the rate range at 8.50% to 10.50% APR.

Interest-only payments are required during the draw period. Annual review is required. A $250 annual fee applies.

### SBA Loans

Emerie First Bank is an SBA Preferred Lender, which means we can process and approve SBA loans in-house for faster turnaround. We offer the following SBA loan programs:

SBA 7(a) Loans: General-purpose business financing for working capital, equipment, real estate, or business acquisition. Loan amounts up to $5,000,000. Terms up to 10 years for working capital and equipment, up to 25 years for real estate.

SBA 504 Loans: Long-term fixed-rate financing for major assets like commercial real estate and heavy equipment. Loan amounts up to $5,500,000 (up to $5,500,000 for manufacturing and energy projects). The borrower typically provides 10% equity, the bank finances 50%, and the CDC (Certified Development Company) finances 40%.

SBA Express Loans: Faster processing for smaller loan amounts up to $500,000. Revolving or term options available.

SBA loan rates vary by program and market conditions. Our business lending team can provide current rate quotes and help determine which program is the best fit.

### Commercial Real Estate Loans

Financing for the purchase, construction, or refinance of owner-occupied and investment commercial properties. Loan amounts range from $100,000 to $5,000,000. Terms range from 5 to 25 years with amortization periods up to 25 years. Rates may be fixed or variable depending on the loan structure.

A minimum down payment of 20% is required for owner-occupied properties and 25% for investment properties. The bank will require a commercial appraisal, environmental assessment (Phase I), and current financial statements from the borrower and any guarantors.

### Equipment Financing

Fixed-rate loans for purchasing business equipment including vehicles, machinery, technology, and furniture. Loan amounts range from $5,000 to $500,000. Terms match the useful life of the equipment, up to 7 years. Current rates start at 7.25% APR. The equipment serves as collateral.

## Treasury Management Services

Emerie First Bank offers treasury management tools for businesses that need advanced cash management capabilities.

Available services include ACH origination (payroll and vendor payments), remote deposit capture (scan and deposit checks from your office), positive pay and payee positive pay (fraud prevention for checks), zero balance accounts, sweep accounts (automatic transfer of excess funds to earn interest), and account analysis statements.

Treasury management services are available to Business Plus and Business Premium Checking account holders. Setup fees and monthly fees vary by service. A treasury management specialist will provide a customized proposal based on the business's needs.

## Merchant Services

Emerie First Bank partners with a third-party payment processor to offer credit card and debit card processing for businesses. Available solutions include point-of-sale terminals, mobile card readers, and e-commerce payment gateways. Rates and fees are based on business type, processing volume, and the chosen equipment. Our business banking team can arrange a consultation with the merchant services provider.

## How to Open a Business Account

Business accounts can be opened in person at any branch location. Online account opening is available for sole proprietorships only. All other business types must visit a branch.

Required documents include articles of organization, articles of incorporation, or partnership agreement (depending on entity type), an Employer Identification Number (EIN) from the IRS (sole proprietors may use their SSN), government-issued photo ID for all signers and beneficial owners, an operating agreement or corporate resolution authorizing signers, and beneficial ownership information for individuals who own 25% or more of the business.

Nonprofit organizations must also provide an IRS determination letter confirming 501(c)(3) status.
