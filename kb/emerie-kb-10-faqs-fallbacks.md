# Emerie First Bank - FAQs and Conversational Fallbacks

## Overview

This module covers frequently asked questions that span multiple product categories, common situational questions, and guidance for handling requests that fall outside the scope of this knowledge base. This is the catch-all reference for topics not fully addressed in the other modules.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

Q: How do I open an account?
A: You can open most personal accounts online at www.emeriefirstbank.com, through the mobile app, or at any branch. Business accounts (other than sole proprietorships) must be opened in person at a branch. You will need a valid government-issued photo ID, your Social Security number or ITIN, and the minimum opening deposit for the account type you choose.

Q: Can I open an account if I am not a U.S. citizen?
A: Yes. Non-U.S. citizens can open an account with a valid passport, an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number (ITIN), and proof of a U.S. residential address. Some account types may have additional requirements. Please visit a branch for assistance.

Q: What is the routing number for Emerie First Bank?
A: The routing number is 111400527. This is used for direct deposits, wire transfers, and ACH transactions.

Q: How do I set up direct deposit?
A: Provide your employer with the Emerie First Bank routing number (111400527) and your checking or savings account number. Your account number can be found in online banking, on the mobile app, or on a check. Most direct deposits begin within one to two pay cycles after setup.

### Account Access and Security

Q: I forgot my online banking password. How do I reset it?
A: Click the Forgot Password link on the login page at www.emeriefirstbank.com or on the mobile app. You will be asked to verify your identity using your username, account number, and the email or phone number on file. If you are unable to reset your password online, call (512) 930-4500 for assistance.

Q: How do I lock my debit card?
A: Open the Emerie First Bank mobile app, go to Card Controls, and toggle the lock switch. You can also call (512) 930-4500 or the 24/7 lost and stolen card line at 1-888-364-7429. Locking the card blocks all new transactions but does not affect recurring automatic payments.

Q: I received a suspicious call or text claiming to be from the bank. What should I do?
A: Do not provide any personal or account information. Emerie First Bank will never call, text, or email you to ask for your full account number, password, PIN, or one-time verification codes. If you receive a suspicious contact, hang up and call us directly at (512) 930-4500 or report it to the fraud line at 1-888-364-7430.

Q: Can someone else access my account?
A: Only authorized signers on the account can access it. You can add an authorized signer by visiting a branch with the person and a valid photo ID. Power of attorney documents can also grant account access and must be reviewed and approved by the bank.

### Everyday Banking

Q: What is my daily ATM withdrawal limit?
A: The daily ATM withdrawal limit is $500 for Basic and Student Checking accounts and $1,000 for Essentials and Premium Checking accounts. You can request a temporary increase by calling customer service or visiting a branch.

Q: How long does a mobile deposit take to clear?
A: The first $225 is available immediately. The remaining funds are typically available the next business day for deposits made before 4:00 PM CT. Larger deposits or deposits to newer accounts may be subject to extended holds.

Q: Does the bank offer cashiers checks?
A: Yes. Cashiers checks are available at any branch for $8.00 each. Premium Checking account holders receive them free of charge.

Q: Can I get change or coins at the branch?
A: Yes. Coin and currency services are available at all branches during lobby hours. Business accounts with high cash volumes should speak with their relationship manager about cash handling services.

Q: Do you offer money orders?
A: Yes. Money orders are available at any branch for $5.00 each.

### Loans and Credit

Q: How do I check the status of my loan application?
A: Call the department that is handling your application. For mortgage loans, call (512) 930-4550. For consumer or business loans, call (512) 930-4500 or (512) 930-4560. Your loan officer can also be reached directly if you have their contact information.

Q: Can I make extra payments on my loan?
A: Yes. Emerie First Bank does not charge a prepayment penalty on any consumer loan, mortgage, or business loan. Extra payments can be made through online banking, by phone, or at a branch. For mortgage loans, specify whether the extra payment should be applied to principal or held for the next payment.

Q: Do you offer credit cards?
A: Emerie First Bank does not currently issue its own credit cards. However, our team can help you explore options through our partner programs. Please visit a branch or call (512) 930-4500 for more information.

Q: Do you offer student loans?
A: Emerie First Bank does not originate student loans. We do offer student loan refinancing through our personal loan products for borrowers who have completed their education. Contact us to discuss whether refinancing might make sense for your situation.

### Account Changes and Life Events

Q: How do I change my name on my account?
A: Visit any branch with a valid government-issued photo ID showing your new name and a legal document supporting the name change (such as a marriage certificate, divorce decree, or court order). Name changes cannot be processed by phone or online.

Q: How do I change my address?
A: You can update your address through online banking, the mobile app, by calling (512) 930-4500, or by visiting any branch. Please update your address before your next statement cycle to ensure delivery.

Q: How do I add or remove someone from my account?
A: Adding or removing a joint account holder requires all current and incoming account holders to visit a branch with valid photo ID. Removing a joint holder requires the consent of all parties on the account.

Q: What happens to my account if I move out of state?
A: You can keep your Emerie First Bank accounts even if you move out of Texas. You will continue to have full access to online banking, mobile banking, Bill Pay, and the Allpoint ATM network nationwide. Branch access will be limited to visits when you are in the area.

## Conversational Fallbacks

The following responses should be used when the chatbot cannot fully answer a question or when the topic requires human assistance.

Response: That is a great question, and I want to make sure you get the most accurate answer. I do not have that specific information available right now. I would recommend contacting our customer service team at (512) 930-4500 or visiting any branch location during business hours. They will be able to help you with that.

### When the customer needs to speak with a specialist

Response: For that type of request, you would benefit from speaking directly with one of our specialists. Would you like me to provide the phone number for the right department, or would you prefer to visit a branch?

Mortgage questions: (512) 930-4550
Business banking questions: (512) 930-4560
General customer service: (512) 930-4500

### When the customer is upset or frustrated

Response: I understand your frustration and I am sorry for the inconvenience. I want to help resolve this for you. For this type of concern, I would recommend speaking with a member of our customer service team directly at (512) 930-4500. They have the tools and authority to review your account and work toward a resolution.

### When the customer asks about a product or service the bank does not offer

Response: Emerie First Bank does not currently offer that product or service. However, we are always expanding our offerings based on customer feedback. I would encourage you to share your interest with our team at (512) 930-4500 or at any branch. In the meantime, I am happy to help you explore the products and services we do offer that might meet your needs.

### When the customer asks for financial advice

Response: I appreciate the question, but I am not able to provide personalized financial, investment, or tax advice. For guidance specific to your situation, I would recommend consulting with a qualified financial advisor or tax professional. I am happy to help you with questions about our banking products, rates, and services.

### When the customer wants to file a complaint

Response: I am sorry to hear about your experience. Your feedback is important to us. You can file a formal complaint by calling (512) 930-4500 and asking to speak with a manager, by visiting any branch and requesting to speak with the branch manager, or by mailing a written complaint to Emerie First Bank, Attn: Customer Relations, 1420 Williams Drive, Georgetown, TX 78628. You may also file a complaint with the Texas Department of Banking at www.dob.texas.gov or the FDIC at www.fdic.gov if you feel your concern has not been adequately addressed.

### When the customer asks about hours or locations

Response: Emerie First Bank has five locations across Central Texas in Georgetown, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Austin, and Temple. Most branches are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Would you like details on a specific branch location, including Saturday hours and drive-through availability?
