# Emerie First Bank - Mortgage Lending

## Overview

Emerie First Bank offers a full suite of residential mortgage products for purchasing or refinancing a home. All mortgage loans are originated and underwritten locally by our in-house lending team. The bank offers both portfolio loans (held in-house) and secondary market loans sold to investors after closing. Borrowers work with the same loan officer from application through closing.

The Mortgage Department can be reached directly at (512) 930-4550.

## Purchase Mortgage Products

### Conventional Fixed-Rate Mortgage

This is the most common mortgage product. It offers a fixed interest rate and fixed monthly payment for the life of the loan.

Available terms are 15, 20, and 30 years. Current rates (as of August 2026):
- 30-year fixed: 6.625% APR
- 20-year fixed: 6.375% APR
- 15-year fixed: 5.875% APR

Minimum down payment is 5% for primary residences. Private mortgage insurance (PMI) is required when the down payment is less than 20%. PMI is automatically removed once the loan-to-value ratio reaches 78% based on the original property value.

Maximum loan amount is $766,550 for conforming loans in the Austin-Round Rock-Georgetown metropolitan area, in line with 2026 Federal Housing Finance Agency limits.

### FHA Loan

FHA loans are insured by the Federal Housing Administration and are designed for borrowers who may not qualify for conventional financing. They offer more flexible credit requirements and lower down payment options.

Minimum down payment is 3.5% with a credit score of 580 or higher. Borrowers with credit scores between 500 and 579 may qualify with a 10% down payment. FHA loans require both an upfront mortgage insurance premium (1.75% of the loan amount, which can be financed into the loan) and an annual mortgage insurance premium paid monthly. The annual premium ranges from 0.45% to 1.05% depending on the loan term, amount, and loan-to-value ratio.

Current 30-year FHA rate: 6.375% APR.

FHA loan limits for the Austin-Round Rock-Georgetown area are $766,550 for a single-family home.

### VA Loan

VA loans are available to eligible active-duty service members, veterans, and surviving spouses. These loans are guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs and offer significant benefits.

No down payment is required. There is no private mortgage insurance. A VA funding fee applies and ranges from 1.25% to 3.30% depending on the borrower's service history, down payment amount, and whether this is a first-time or subsequent use of VA benefits. The funding fee can be financed into the loan. Some borrowers are exempt from the funding fee, including veterans receiving VA disability compensation.

Current 30-year VA rate: 6.25% APR.

There is no maximum loan amount for borrowers with full entitlement. Borrowers with reduced entitlement may be subject to county loan limits.

Emerie First Bank requires a Certificate of Eligibility from the VA. Our loan officers can help borrowers request one through the VA's online portal.

### Jumbo Loan

Jumbo loans are for loan amounts that exceed the conforming loan limit. Emerie First Bank offers jumbo financing for primary residences and second homes.

Loan amounts range from $766,551 to $2,000,000. Available terms are 15 and 30 years. Minimum down payment is 20%. A credit score of 700 or higher is generally required.

Current 30-year jumbo rate: 6.875% APR.
Current 15-year jumbo rate: 6.125% APR.

Jumbo loans are held in the bank's portfolio and are not sold on the secondary market.

### USDA Rural Development Loan

USDA loans are available for properties in eligible rural areas as defined by the U.S. Department of Agriculture. Parts of Williamson County, Bell County, and surrounding areas may qualify.

No down payment is required. Income limits apply based on household size and county. USDA loans require a guarantee fee of 1.00% upfront (can be financed) and an annual fee of 0.35% paid monthly.

Current 30-year USDA rate: 6.375% APR.

Borrowers can check property and income eligibility on the USDA website. Our loan officers can assist with eligibility determination.

## Refinance Options

### Rate-and-Term Refinance

This option replaces an existing mortgage with a new loan at a different rate or term without taking cash out. It is commonly used to lower the monthly payment, shorten the loan term, or switch from an adjustable rate to a fixed rate.

All mortgage product types listed above are available for rate-and-term refinancing. The same rates, terms, and qualification requirements apply.

### Cash-Out Refinance

A cash-out refinance replaces the existing mortgage with a new, larger loan, and the borrower receives the difference in cash. This can be used for home improvements, debt consolidation, or other purposes.

Maximum loan-to-value ratio for cash-out refinance is 80% for conventional loans and 85% for FHA loans. VA cash-out refinance allows up to 90% loan-to-value. Cash-out refinance rates are typically 0.125% to 0.250% higher than standard rate-and-term refinance rates.

### Streamline Refinance

FHA Streamline and VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) options are available for borrowers with existing FHA or VA loans. These programs offer reduced documentation requirements and may not require a new appraisal.

## The Mortgage Process

The typical mortgage process at Emerie First Bank takes 30 to 45 days from application to closing. Here is what to expect:

Step 1: Prequalification or preapproval. Borrowers can get prequalified online or by phone with no impact to their credit score. A full preapproval requires a credit check and documentation review.

Step 2: Application. Borrowers submit a complete application along with required documents including two years of tax returns, two months of bank statements, recent pay stubs, and identification.

Step 3: Processing and underwriting. The bank orders an appraisal, verifies employment and assets, and reviews the full file. Borrowers may be asked for additional documentation during this stage.

Step 4: Conditional approval. The underwriter issues a conditional approval with a list of any remaining items needed before final approval.

Step 5: Clear to close. Once all conditions are satisfied, the loan is cleared to close. The borrower receives a Closing Disclosure at least three business days before the scheduled closing date.

Step 6: Closing. The borrower signs final documents at a title company or attorney's office. Funds are disbursed and the transaction is complete.

## Down Payment Assistance

Emerie First Bank participates in the Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation (TSAHC) and Southeast Texas Housing Finance Corporation down payment assistance programs. These programs offer grants or forgivable second liens to help qualifying borrowers cover down payment and closing costs. Eligibility is based on income limits, credit score, and property location. Our loan officers can help determine eligibility and guide borrowers through the application process.

## General Mortgage Information

A $500 application fee applies to all mortgage applications. This fee is credited toward closing costs at closing. If the loan does not close, the application fee is nonrefundable.

Closing costs typically range from 2% to 5% of the loan amount and include the appraisal, title insurance, escrow reserves, recording fees, and lender fees. A detailed Loan Estimate is provided within three business days of receiving a complete application.

Emerie First Bank does not charge a prepayment penalty on any residential mortgage product.
