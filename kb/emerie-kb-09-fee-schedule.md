# Emerie First Bank - Fee Schedule

## Overview

This is a consolidated reference of fees charged by Emerie First Bank across all account types and services. All fees are subject to change with 30 days advance notice to the customer. Fees are current as of March 2026.

## Account Maintenance Fees

EFB Basic Checking: No monthly fee.
EFB Essentials Checking: $8.95 per month, waived with $1,500 minimum daily balance or $500 in qualifying direct deposits.
EFB Premium Checking: $18.00 per month, waived with $10,000 minimum daily balance or $25,000 in combined deposit and loan balances.
EFB Student Checking: No monthly fee.
EFB Standard Savings: $5.00 per month, waived with $300 minimum daily balance or account holder under 18.
EFB High-Yield Savings: $12.00 per month, waived with $5,000 minimum daily balance.
EFB Money Market: $10.00 per month, waived with $10,000 minimum daily balance.
EFB Business Basic Checking: No monthly fee.
EFB Business Plus Checking: $20.00 per month, waived with $10,000 minimum daily balance.
EFB Business Premium Checking: $35.00 per month, waived with $50,000 minimum daily balance or $100,000 combined business deposit and loan balances.
EFB Nonprofit Checking: No monthly fee.
Business Savings: $5.00 per month, waived with $1,000 minimum daily balance.
Business Money Market: $15.00 per month, waived with $25,000 minimum daily balance.

## Overdraft and Insufficient Funds Fees

Standard Overdraft Fee (per item): $29.00, maximum of three per business day ($87.00 daily cap).
Small Item Exemption: Transactions of $5.00 or less do not trigger an overdraft fee.
Overdraft Protection Transfer Fee (from linked savings or line of credit): $5.00 per transfer.
Returned Item Fee (NSF, item returned unpaid): $29.00 per item.
Continuous Overdraft Fee: None. Emerie First Bank does not charge a daily fee for accounts that remain overdrawn.

## ATM Fees

Emerie First Bank ATMs: No fee for customers.
Allpoint Network ATMs: No surcharge for customers.
Non-network ATMs (domestic): $2.50 per transaction. The ATM operator may also charge a fee.
Non-network ATMs (international): $3.50 per transaction plus 1.00% foreign transaction fee on the withdrawal amount.

## Debit Card Fees

Replacement debit card (standard, 7 to 10 business days): No fee.
Rush replacement debit card (2 to 3 business days): $15.00.
Foreign transaction fee: 1.00% of the transaction amount. Waived for Premium Checking.

## Wire Transfer Fees

Domestic outgoing: $25.00. Waived for Premium Checking (up to 2 per month) and Business Premium Checking (unlimited).
Domestic incoming: $10.00. Waived for Essentials, Premium, Business Plus, and Business Premium Checking.
International outgoing: $45.00.
International incoming: $15.00.

## Check and Paper Item Fees

Cashiers check: $8.00 each. Free for Premium Checking (unlimited) and Essentials Checking (up to 3 per month).
Personal check printing (standard, box of 100): $22.00. Duplicate checks: $28.00.
Business check printing (standard, box of 250): $45.00.
Counter checks (temporary, per sheet of 3): $2.00.

## Statement and Document Fees

eStatements: Free for all accounts.
Paper statements (personal accounts): Free.
Paper statements (business accounts): $5.00 per month.
Duplicate statement request: $5.00 per statement.
Account verification letter: $10.00.
Audit confirmation letter: $25.00.

## Account Service Fees

Stop payment (per item): $30.00, effective for six months.
Account reconciliation (business accounts): $0.10 per item.
Early account closure (within 90 days of opening): $25.00.
Dormant account fee (after 12 months of no activity): $5.00 per month.
Garnishment or levy processing fee: $100.00 per order.
Legal process or subpoena fee: $75.00 per request.
Check image request (beyond online banking): $3.00 per image.

## Safe Deposit Box Fees

Small box (3 x 5 inches): $50.00 per year.
Medium box (5 x 10 inches): $100.00 per year.
Large box (10 x 10 inches): $175.00 per year.
Lost key replacement: $25.00 per key.
Box drilling (both keys lost): $150.00 to $250.00.
Late rental payment fee: $15.00.

## Savings and Money Market Transaction Fees

Excess withdrawal fee (Savings accounts, beyond 6 per cycle): $2.00 per transaction.
Excess withdrawal fee (Money Market, beyond 6 per cycle): $10.00 per transaction.

## Cash Handling Fees (Business Accounts)

Business Basic Checking: $1.50 per $1,000 over $5,000 per month.
Business Plus Checking: $1.50 per $1,000 over $10,000 per month.
Business Premium Checking: $1.00 per $1,000 over $25,000 per month.
Coin counting and wrapping: $5.00 per bag.

## Lending Fees

Mortgage application fee: $500.00, credited toward closing costs.
Auto and RV loan documentation fee: $150.00.
Personal loan late payment fee: $25.00 (more than 10 days past due).
Mortgage late payment fee: 5% of past-due amount (more than 15 days past due).
Business line of credit annual fee: $250.00.
HELOC annual fee: $75.00, waived first year.
HELOC rate lock fee: $100.00.

## Miscellaneous Fees

Notary services: Free for Emerie First Bank customers.
Money order: $5.00 each.
Gift card (Visa branded): $5.95 per card.
Coin counting for non-customers: $10.00 per bag or 5% of total, whichever is greater.
Fax service: $2.00 per page domestic, $5.00 per page international.
Research fee (history beyond 7 years or complex requests): $25.00 per hour, one hour minimum.
