# Emerie First Bank - Personal Checking Accounts

## Overview

Emerie First Bank offers four personal checking account options designed to fit different banking needs. All checking accounts include a Visa debit card, access to online and mobile banking, and free Bill Pay.

## Account Types

### EFB Basic Checking

This is our no-frills checking account with no minimum balance requirement and no monthly maintenance fee. It is a good fit for customers who want straightforward checking without extras.

Features include a Visa debit card, online and mobile banking, mobile deposit, one free official bank check per month, and access to the Allpoint surcharge-free ATM network. Paper statements are mailed at no charge, or customers can switch to eStatements.

There is no minimum opening deposit requirement beyond $25 to open the account.

### EFB Essentials Checking

This account is designed for everyday banking with a few added benefits. The monthly maintenance fee is $8.95, but it is waived when the account maintains a $1,500 minimum daily balance or receives at least $500 in qualifying direct deposits per month.

In addition to everything included with Basic Checking, Essentials Checking adds free official bank checks (up to three per month), free incoming domestic wire transfers, and a small interest rate of 0.05% APY on balances over $1,000.

The minimum opening deposit is $100.

### EFB Premium Checking

Premium Checking is built for customers who maintain higher balances and want more benefits. The monthly maintenance fee is $18.00, waived with a $10,000 minimum daily balance or $25,000 in combined deposit and loan balances at the bank.

Premium Checking includes everything in Essentials Checking plus a higher interest rate of 0.15% APY on balances over $5,000, unlimited free official bank checks, free outgoing domestic wire transfers (up to two per month), no foreign transaction fees on debit card purchases, and a free small safe deposit box (subject to availability at your branch).

The minimum opening deposit is $500.

### EFB Student Checking

Student Checking is available to customers between the ages of 16 and 24 who are enrolled in high school, college, or a vocational program. There is no monthly maintenance fee and no minimum balance requirement.

Student Checking includes a Visa debit card, online and mobile banking, mobile deposit, and free eStatements. Paper statements are available for $3.00 per month. The account automatically converts to EFB Basic Checking when the account holder turns 25 or is no longer a student, whichever comes first.

The minimum opening deposit is $25. A parent or legal guardian must be a joint account holder for customers under 18.

## Debit Card Details

All checking accounts come with an Emerie First Bank Visa debit card. Daily purchase limits and ATM withdrawal limits are as follows:

Daily point-of-sale purchase limit: $3,000 for Basic and Student accounts, $5,000 for Essentials and Premium accounts.
Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $500 for Basic and Student accounts, $1,000 for Essentials and Premium accounts.

Customers can request temporary limit increases by calling customer service or visiting a branch. Temporary increases are available for up to 7 days.

Debit cards include Visa Zero Liability protection and can be added to Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay. Card controls are available through the Emerie First Bank mobile app, allowing customers to lock and unlock their card, set transaction alerts, and restrict certain purchase categories.

## Overdraft Options

Emerie First Bank offers three ways to handle overdrafts:

**Overdraft Protection Transfer:** Link your checking account to an Emerie First Bank savings account or line of credit. If a transaction would overdraw your checking account, funds are automatically transferred in $100 increments. The transfer fee is $5.00 per transfer from a savings account or line of credit.

**Standard Overdraft Coverage:** The bank may authorize and pay overdrafts on checks and recurring debit transactions at its discretion. Each overdraft item is subject to a $29.00 fee, with a maximum of three overdraft fees per business day ($87.00 daily cap). Items of $5.00 or less will not trigger an overdraft fee.

**No Overdraft Coverage:** Customers may opt out of all overdraft services. Transactions that would overdraw the account will simply be declined at no charge.

All customers are automatically enrolled in No Overdraft Coverage when they open an account. Customers must opt in to Standard Overdraft Coverage or set up Overdraft Protection Transfer.

## How to Open an Account

Customers can open a personal checking account online at www.emeriefirstbank.com, through the Emerie First Bank mobile app, or in person at any branch location. Required documents include a valid government-issued photo ID (such as a driver's license or passport), a Social Security number or Individual Taxpayer Identification Number, and the minimum opening deposit.

Joint accounts require identification and Social Security numbers for all account holders. At least one account holder must be present in person or complete the online application.
