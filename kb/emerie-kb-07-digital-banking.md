# Emerie First Bank - Digital Banking and Technology

## Overview

Emerie First Bank offers a full suite of digital banking tools so customers can manage their accounts anytime, anywhere. All digital banking services are free for personal and business account holders.

## Online Banking

Online banking is available at www.emeriefirstbank.com and provides access to all linked accounts including checking, savings, money market, CDs, loans, and lines of credit.

Features include real-time balance and transaction viewing, account-to-account transfers between Emerie First Bank accounts, external transfers to and from accounts at other banks (typically 1 to 3 business days), Bill Pay with the ability to schedule one-time and recurring payments, eStatements with up to 7 years of statement history, transaction search and filtering, account alerts (balance thresholds, large transactions, direct deposit received), and the ability to download transactions in CSV, OFX, or QFX format for use with accounting software like QuickBooks.

To enroll, customers need their account number and the last four digits of their Social Security number. A username and password are created during enrollment. Two-factor authentication is required and can be set up with SMS text codes or an authenticator app.

## Mobile Banking App

The Emerie First Bank mobile app is available for iPhone (iOS 15 and later) and Android (version 10 and later). It can be downloaded from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store by searching for Emerie First Bank.

The app includes all features available in online banking plus the following:

Mobile Deposit: Deposit checks by taking a photo with your phone camera. Personal account holders can deposit up to $5,000 per day and $15,000 per rolling 30-day period. Business account holders can deposit up to $10,000 per day and $25,000 per rolling 30-day period. Deposits made before 4:00 PM CT on a business day are typically available the next business day. The first $225 is available immediately.

Biometric Login: Sign in using Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint depending on your device.

Card Controls: Lock and unlock your debit card instantly, set spending limits by category, restrict transactions by geographic region, and turn international transactions on or off.

Push Notifications: Real-time alerts for transactions, deposits, and security events.

ATM Locator: Find Emerie First Bank and Allpoint surcharge-free ATMs near your current location.

Secure Messaging: Send and receive encrypted messages with the customer service team directly through the app.

## Zelle

Emerie First Bank supports Zelle for person-to-person payments. Zelle is built into the mobile app and online banking platform. Customers can send money to anyone with a U.S. bank account using the recipient email address or mobile phone number.

Sending limits for personal accounts are $1,000 per transaction and $2,500 per day. Business accounts have limits of $5,000 per transaction and $10,000 per day. Transfers between enrolled Zelle users are typically delivered within minutes.

There is no fee to send or receive money through Zelle. Customers should only send money to people they know and trust, as Zelle payments cannot be reversed once the recipient has enrolled.

## Bill Pay

Bill Pay is included with all checking accounts through online and mobile banking. Customers can pay virtually any company or individual in the United States.

Payments to major billers (utilities, credit cards, insurance companies) are sent electronically and typically arrive within 1 to 2 business days. Payments to smaller companies or individuals are sent by check and may take 5 to 7 business days.

Customers can schedule one-time payments, set up recurring payments on a weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedule, and receive eBills from participating billers. There is no per-payment fee for Bill Pay.

## External Transfers

Customers can link accounts at other financial institutions to their Emerie First Bank online banking profile. This allows transfers in either direction. Linking an external account requires micro-deposit verification, which takes 1 to 3 business days. Once verified, transfers typically settle in 1 to 3 business days.

Transfer limits are $5,000 per transaction and $10,000 per day for personal accounts. Business accounts can request higher limits through their relationship manager.

There is no fee for external transfers.

## Account Alerts

Customers can set up customizable alerts through online banking or the mobile app. Alerts can be delivered by email, push notification, or SMS text message.

Available alert types include balance falling below a set threshold, balance exceeding a set threshold, transactions over a set dollar amount, direct deposit received, check cleared, debit card transaction, loan payment due reminder, and CD maturity reminder.

Alerts are informational and do not block or stop transactions.

## eStatements

eStatements are free for all account types and are the default delivery method for new accounts. Statements are available in online banking within one business day of the statement cycle closing. Customers can view, download, and print statements going back 7 years.

Customers who prefer paper statements can request them at no charge for personal checking and savings accounts. Business accounts are charged $5.00 per month for paper statements.

## Security Features

Emerie First Bank uses multiple layers of security to protect digital banking.

Two-factor authentication is required for login and can use SMS codes or an authenticator app. Device recognition flags logins from new or unrecognized devices and requires additional verification. Session timeout automatically logs customers out after 10 minutes of inactivity. All data transmitted between the customer and the bank is protected with 256-bit SSL encryption.

If a customer suspects unauthorized access to their online or mobile banking, they should call (512) 930-4500 or the 24/7 fraud line at 1-888-364-7430 immediately. The bank can lock online access and the associated debit card while the situation is investigated.

## Digital Banking Support

For help with online banking enrollment, password resets, mobile app issues, or any digital banking question, customers can call (512) 930-4500 during regular business hours, use secure messaging through the app, or visit any branch for in-person assistance. Password resets can also be done online through the Forgot Password link on the login page.
